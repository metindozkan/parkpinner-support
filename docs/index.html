❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my parking location?

Tap the "I Parked Here" button on the home screen. The app will capture your GPS location and allow you to take a photo and add details like floor, section, and notes.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. This will restore any previous purchases linked to your Apple ID.

Can I export my parking history?

Yes! Premium users can export their parking history as a CSV file. Go to Settings → Export & Delete Data → Export History.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Export & Delete Data. You can delete your entire parking history or individual sessions from the History screen.

Why aren't my meter reminders working?

Make sure you've granted notification permissions to ParkPinner in your iPhone Settings. Also check that you've set a meter expiration time when saving your parking spot.

What's included in Premium?

Premium includes unlimited parking history (free users get 5), expense tracking, no ads, all premium themes, and data export functionality. You can try Premium free for 7 days!

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → ParkPinner. You'll keep Premium access until the end of your billing period.

The app isn't getting my location. What should I do?

Make sure Location Services are enabled for ParkPinner in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ParkPinner → While Using the App.

🔒 Privacy & Data

Your privacy matters to us. All your parking data, photos, and location information are stored locally on your device only. We never upload your data to external servers.

What we collect:

• Parking locations (GPS coordinates) - stored locally
• Photos of parking spots - stored locally
• User preferences and settings - stored locally
• Anonymous usage data via AdMob (free users only)

What we DON'T collect:

• We don't sell your data
• We don't share your personal information
• We don't track your location when the app is closed
• We don't store data on external servers

Third-Party Services:

• Google AdMob: Shows ads to free users. AdMob may collect device information and ad interaction data.
• Apple In-App Purchase: Handles Premium subscriptions and purchases.
