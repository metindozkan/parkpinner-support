Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

TL;DR: All your data stays on your device. We don't upload, sell, or share your personal information. We only use Google AdMob for ads (free version only), and Apple for payments.

Introduction

Welcome to ParkPinner! This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Developer: Apexify
Contact: apexifylabs@gmail.com

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Parking Locations: GPS coordinates of where you parked
  • Photos: Pictures you take of your parking spots
  • Parking Details: Information you enter such as floor number, section, spot number, notes, and parking costs
  • Parking History: Records of your past parking sessions
  • Favorite Locations: Locations you save as favorites (Premium feature)
  • App Preferences: Your settings, theme choices, and preferences
  • Purchase History: Records of your in-app purchases (managed by Apple)

Important: We DO NOT have access to any of this data. It stays entirely on your iPhone and is protected by iOS security.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or personal information
  • We do not track your location when the app is closed
  • We do not upload your photos or parking data to any server
  • We do not share your data with third parties (except as noted below for ads)
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles

How We Use Your Information

Core Functionality

The data stored locally on your device is used exclusively for:

  • Displaying your current parking location and details
  • Providing navigation directions to your parked car
  • Showing your parking history
  • Setting and triggering meter expiration reminders
  • Managing favorite parking locations
  • Tracking parking expenses (Premium feature)
  • Exporting your parking data (Premium feature)

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Applies to: Free users only (not Premium subscribers or users who purchased "Remove Ads")

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device information (device type, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads you view or click)
  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Advertising ID (can be reset in iOS Settings)

You can control ad tracking: Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and disable tracking for ParkPinner.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Apple In-App Purchase

We use Apple's In-App Purchase system for:

  • Premium subscriptions (monthly and yearly)
  • One-time purchases (Remove Ads, Themes Pack)

Apple processes all payments and manages subscription data. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, not your payment information.

Learn more: Apple Privacy Policy

Apple Maps

When you use the "Navigate to Car" feature, the app opens Apple Maps. Location data is handled according to Apple's privacy policy.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All your parking data is stored in the app's private storage on your device using:

  • Encrypted Storage: iOS protects your data with device encryption
  • No Cloud Sync: Data is not synced to iCloud or any cloud service
  • App Sandbox: Your data is isolated from other apps

Photos

Photos you take are stored in the app's private directory and are:

  • Not visible in your iPhone Photos app
  • Not backed up to iCloud Photos
  • Deleted when you delete a parking session or uninstall the app

Permissions We Request

ParkPinner requests the following iOS permissions:

Location (Required)

  • When Requested: When you tap "I Parked Here"
  • Why: To capture your current GPS location
  • Access Level: "While Using the App" only

Camera (Required)

  • When Requested: When you take a photo of your parking spot
  • Why: To capture photos of your parking location

Photo Library (Optional)

  • When Requested: When you want to select existing photos
  • Why: To allow choosing photos from your library (Premium feature - multiple photos)

Notifications (Optional)

  • When Requested: When you set a meter expiration time
  • Why: To remind you before your parking meter expires

Tracking (Optional - for Ads)

  • When Requested: On first app launch (free users only)
  • Why: To show personalized ads via AdMob
  • You can decline: Declining will still show ads, but they won't be personalized

Data Deletion

Delete Individual Sessions

You can delete individual parking sessions by swiping left on any session in the History screen and tapping "Delete".

Delete All Data

To delete all your data:

  1. Open ParkPinner
  2. Go to Settings → Export & Delete Data
  3. Tap Delete All History
  4. Confirm deletion

Uninstalling the App

When you delete ParkPinner from your iPhone, all locally stored data (parking history, photos, preferences) is permanently deleted. This data cannot be recovered.

Children's Privacy

ParkPinner is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The app is designed for licensed drivers who are typically 16 years or older.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify users through an in-app message for significant changes

Continued use of ParkPinner after changes means you accept the updated policy.

International Users

ParkPinner is available worldwide. All data processing happens locally on your device, regardless of your location. No data is transmitted to servers in any country.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All your data is visible in the app (History, Favorites, Settings)
  • Export your data: Premium users can export parking history as CSV
  • Delete your data: Delete individual sessions or all data at any time
  • Control permissions: Manage app permissions in iOS Settings
  • Opt-out of tracking: Disable ad tracking in iOS Settings
